📜 How Wizard works

  • 60 cards: a normal 52-card deck + 4 Wizards + 4 Jesters. Round k deals k cards each (60 ÷ players rounds).
  • The next card is flipped for trump. Jester → no trump; Wizard → the dealer picks. In the last round no card is left, so there's no trump.
  • Everyone bids exactly how many tricks they'll take, starting left of the dealer.
  • You must follow the led suit if you can — but a Wizard or Jester may always be played.
  • First Wizard wins the trick; otherwise highest trump; otherwise highest card of the led suit. Jesters always lose (unless all Jesters).
  • Exact bid: 20 + 10 per trick. Miss: −10 per trick off, over or under.
