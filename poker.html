📋 Optimal strategy (memorize this)

Pre-flop
bet 4×		 Raise 4× with: any pair 3-3+, any Ace, K (any suited / K5o+), Q (Q6s+ / Q8o+), J (J8s+ / JTo+). Otherwise check. 3× is always worse than 4× — never use it.
Flop
bet 2×		 Bet 2× with two pair or better, a hidden pair (you paired a hole card), or 4-to-a-flush with a hidden 10+. Otherwise check.
River
bet 1× / fold		 Bet 1× unless the dealer beats you too often — trainer computes this exactly (all 990 dealer hands). Rule of thumb: raise with a hidden pair or better; fold when your hole cards don't beat the board.
